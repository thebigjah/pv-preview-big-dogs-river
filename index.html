We rent kayaks and tubes on the Middle Oconee River. A shuttle takes you 15 minutes upstream, then you float 3.5 miles back to your vehicle at your own pace.

Tallassee Shoals at Mitchell Bridge

The most popular stretch for kayakers. It runs Class I-II depending on water level, so call to check weather conditions.

Paddlers go in sit-on-top kayaks, single or tandem. The Middle Oconee runs on through Athens to the Oconee River.

One price covers the whole trip.

$50Kayaking, per person
$40Tubing, per person

The rate includes:

  • Parking
  • Equipment
  • Shuttle service

Bring your own boat and the same rate applies. We transport it for you. Each participant needs a signed release waiver.

Monday to Friday (holidays too) and Saturday before 12pm. Saturday: call ahead for reservations.

Your boat

  • Sit-on-top kayaks, single and tandem
  • Lightweight aluminum paddles
  • PFDs with pockets for storage
  • Bring sunscreen, footwear and fluids

On the river

  • No one goes on the river alone
  • No Styrofoam or glass
  • A small cooler is allowed for water only
  • Call to check weather conditions

Up the hill is the Cabana.

When you come off the river, an indoor and outdoor space is waiting with grills, a restroom, picnic tables and satellite TV. Some weekends there is live music.

VolleyballHorseshoesYard games

The Cabana also rents for birthdays, weddings and parties, with transportation provided. Call for details.

Group packages

Door to door round-trip shuttle for local groups. Add a band and food to customize your party.

  • Team building
  • Social events
  • Private parties
  • Family outings
  • Fundraising and recruitment

Shuttles run Sundays for large group reservations only. Call 706.353.6002

Family owned since 2010

T.J. Stephens and his father Terry Stephens started Big Dogs on the River. Their mission is a better, cleaner Middle Oconee River and surrounding streams, by educating people and having them enjoy Athens' natural resources.
